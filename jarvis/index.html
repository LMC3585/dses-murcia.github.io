Connection checklist
  1. Home Assistant must be reachable over HTTPS from your phone (Home Assistant Cloud or Tailscale).
  2. Add this site to http.cors_allowed_origins in configuration.yaml and restart HA.
  3. Install the Anthropic integration in HA and enable it as a voice assistant with "Control Home Assistant" on.
